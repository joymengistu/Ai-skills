# UI/UX research notes

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 is a W3C Recommendation. Relevant design and interaction requirements include sufficient text contrast, keyboard accessibility, visible focus, target size, reflow, non-text contrast, content on hover or focus, and pause/stop/hide behavior.[1] These are quality gates, not optional polish.

## Visual hierarchy

Nielsen Norman Group describes visual hierarchy as guiding attention through color/contrast, scale, and grouping. The practical implications are to reserve stronger contrast and larger scale for important elements, use proximity and whitespace to establish relationships, limit competing emphasis, and avoid relying on color alone.[2] Its squint-test framing supports the user's request to inspect whether a screen's primary action remains obvious when details are visually blurred.

MASTER RESEARCH MISSION
AI SKILLS REPOSITORY — MAXIMUM IMPROVEMENT PASS

You are operating on an existing AI Skills repository.

This is a LONG-RUNNING RESEARCH + ENGINEERING mission.

Do NOT treat this as a normal question-answering task.

Your job is to investigate, experiment, critique, benchmark, improve,
and document the repository.

The objective is NOT to create the largest number of Skills.

The objective is to create the HIGHEST-QUALITY SKILL SYSTEM that can
reasonably be built from available evidence and tooling.

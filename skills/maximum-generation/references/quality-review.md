# Quality Review

## Select relevant dimensions

Do not use every dimension on every artifact. Score only what the artifact can meaningfully demonstrate, then retain the evidence, confidence, and observation method for each score.

| Dimension family | Inspect when relevant |
|---|---|
| Visual | Composition, hierarchy, color/material coherence, depth, detail purpose, consistency, originality. |
| Experience | First impression, clarity, curiosity, emotional tone, memorability, pacing, discovery. |
| Interaction | Responsiveness, feedback, motion purpose, transitions, physicality, sound where present. |
| Product | Coherence, usability, accessibility, responsiveness, performance, reliability, recovery. |
| Generative | Variety, non-repetition, meaningful variation, procedural coherence, avoidance of generic patterns. |

## Score interpretation

| Score | Meaning | Required response |
|---:|---|---|
| 1–3 | Broken, incoherent, or substantially misses intent. | Repair a hard cause before adding polish. |
| 4–5 | Functional but generic, unclear, repetitive, or weak in the selected dimension. | Targeted improvement is required before a strong-quality claim. |
| 6–7 | Intentional and usable with visible limitations. | Improve only the highest-value weakness; partial qualification may be appropriate. |
| 8–9 | Strong evidence of a coherent, differentiated outcome in the assessed dimensions. | Stop unless a clear high-value improvement remains. |
| 10 | Exceptional in context, supported by unusually strong evidence and preferably human review. | Do not assign by default or from self-praise. |

Use a domain-adjusted threshold. A practical default is: every hard requirement passes, no required quality dimension is below 6, and the selected review has no unresolved generic-pattern or usability blocker. Do not average away a critical weakness.

## Anti-generic review

Look for default layouts, repeated geometry, arbitrary gradients, unused decorative elements, excessive cards, predictable palettes, lifeless scenes, fake complexity, repeated procedural structures, unclear hierarchy, or motion without purpose. Ask: **What makes this intentional for this person and this outcome?** Then change the smallest meaningful cause.

## Evidence record

For each important score, record the observation, source, confidence, scope, and limitation. A screenshot can show composition but not keyboard flow; a passing test can show a state transition but not memorability; a human reaction can inform preference but is not a performance benchmark.
